# Kumotori-yama traverse from Mitsumine

Left the Mitsumine shrine at 06:20 under a clear sky. The ridge was quiet until
Kirimogamine; the first hikers coming the other way appeared below Shiraiwa.

:::waypoint{name="Kirimogamine" latitude="35.900" longitude="138.950" elevation_m=1523 arrived_at="2026-07-10T08:05:00+09:00"}
Good view back to the shrine. Bench and a signpost.
:::

:::hazard{severity="warning" latitude="35.878" longitude="138.941" observed_at="2026-07-10T09:30:00+09:00"}
A fallen tree blocks the trail just below Shiraiwa hut. It can be passed on the
right, but the ground drops away steeply there — take it slowly with a big pack.
:::

:::trail-condition{status="caution" section="Shiraiwa hut – Kumotori summit"}
Mud and a few leftover snow patches on the north side of Kumotori. Trail runners
were fine; a light pair of spikes would have been comfortable.
:::

:::waypoint{name="Kumotori-yama" latitude="35.855" longitude="138.944" elevation_m=2017 arrived_at="2026-07-10T11:40:00+09:00" departed_at="2026-07-10T12:20:00+09:00"}
Lunch on the summit. Fuji visible above a thin layer of cloud.
:::

The descent to Kamosawa is long but gentle. Water at the Kumotori hut tap was
running.

:::hazard{severity="info" latitude="35.843" longitude="138.957"}
The signpost at the Nanatsuishi junction is missing its Kamosawa arm; keep
right (downhill) at the fork.
:::
